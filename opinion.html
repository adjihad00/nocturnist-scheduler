Alternative Scheduling Models

Rate each alternative and tell us what you prefer.

Model A: Block Template (Quarterly/Trimester)

Template-Based Shorter Commitment Requests Preserved
  • Year divided into 3 or 4 blocks
  • Preferences submitted in advance of each block
  • Reduced HPRs (e.g., 5/month) but still available
  • Vacation days allocated per block proportionally
  • Template can vary block-to-block based on group needs

Model B: Team-Based Self-Scheduling

Team Structure Self-Schedule Coverage Guaranteed
  • Nocturnists divided into staggered teams for coverage
  • Each team has a template slot; self-schedule into your assigned slots
  • Trades/swaps managed within and across teams
  • Generalist coverage built around nocturnist template

Model C: Graduated Transition (Phased)

Phased Preserves Current 12-Month Rollout
  • Phase 1 (Mo 1-3): Current system maintained. Software upgraded. HPRs 10 → 8.
  • Phase 2 (Mo 4-6): 1 anchor block/quarter. Self-schedule the rest. HPRs at 6.
  • Phase 3 (Mo 7-9): 2 anchor blocks/quarter. HPRs at 4. Coverage review.
  • Phase 4 (Mo 10-12): Full assessment. Group votes to continue, hold, or revert.
  • Built-in off-ramp: group can halt at any phase

Model D: Enhanced Status Quo (No Template)

Current System Software Upgrade Coverage Incentives
  • Keep current self-scheduling with HPRs and vacation intact
  • Invest in better scheduling software
  • Nocturnist schedule published earlier
  • Add coverage incentives for hard-to-fill shifts
  • Algorithm-assisted schedule optimization
